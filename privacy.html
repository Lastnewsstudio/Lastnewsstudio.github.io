Privacy Policy — MARCH理系の英語塾 Uploader

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "MARCH理系の英語塾 Uploader" (the "App") handles information. The App is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube / TikTok channel "MARCH理系の英語塾" (the "Operator"). It is used only to upload short educational videos created by the Operator to the Operator's own TikTok account.

1. Who uses the App

The App is used only by the Operator. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in.

2. Information we collect

When the Operator connects the App to TikTok with TikTok Login Kit, the App receives:

  • An access token and a refresh token issued by TikTok
  • Basic account information provided by the user.info.basic scope (such as the account's open ID)

The App does not collect information about viewers, followers, or any other TikTok users.

3. How we use the information

The tokens are used only to upload videos made by the Operator to the Operator's TikTok inbox (drafts) with the video.upload scope. The Operator reviews and publishes each video in the TikTok app.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the Operator's private GitHub repository (GitHub Actions secrets) and are used only while the upload job runs. They are not shared, sold, or used for advertising.

5. Sharing

We do not share information with third parties, except as required to call the TikTok API itself.

6. Deleting data / revoking access

The Operator can revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Apps). When access is revoked, the stored tokens are deleted.
